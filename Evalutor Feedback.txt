This project shows the healthcare operations dashboard for analysing the CBP Custody then HHS Care and then sponsor placement pipeline. The project tracks transfer efficiency, discharge performance, backlog levels, placement outcomes, and operational bottlenecks over the selected period. The dashboard reports a 69.10% transfer efficiency and 2.37% discharge efficiency, while also detecting a high backlog of 2,502 cases. The use of pipeline flow visualisation, efficiency trends, bottleneck detection, backlog accumulation, monthly placement analysis, and sudden placement-drop identification. For improvements, the project requires improvement in KPI calculation and data validation, as Pipeline Throughput and Placement Ratio display inf%, which indicates a division-by-zero or invalid denominator issue. Placement ratios above 100% and even 1,000% should also be reviewed to confirm whether the metric definition and aggregation logic are correct.
