# Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics** project analyzes the operational efficiency of a multi-stage care and reunification pipeline.

The system models the movement of children through three critical stages:

**CBP Custody → HHS Care → Sponsor Placement**

The objective is to identify transition delays, measure process efficiency, detect bottlenecks, and analyze placement outcomes using data analytics and interactive visualization.

This project transforms raw operational data into actionable insights for improving workflow efficiency, reducing case backlogs, and supporting faster reunification processes.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The UAC (Unaccompanied Children) care system operates as a multi-stage pipeline involving:

1. Apprehension and CBP custody
2. Transfer to HHS care
3. Medical screening, sheltering, and case management
4. Discharge and sponsor reunification

While total custody numbers are monitored, process efficiency indicators are often difficult to evaluate.

Key unanswered questions:

- How efficiently are children transferred from CBP to HHS?
- Are discharge rates keeping pace with new arrivals?
- Where do care backlogs accumulate?
- Are placement outcomes improving or declining over time?

This project addresses these questions through structured transition analytics.

---

# 🚀 Project Objectives

## Primary Objectives

- Measure CBP → HHS transition efficiency
- Evaluate discharge and sponsor placement performance
- Identify operational bottlenecks
- Analyze pipeline flow and backlog accumulation
- Monitor placement outcome trends


## Secondary Objectives

- Support faster reunification workflows
- Improve case management visibility
- Provide data-driven insights for operational decisions
